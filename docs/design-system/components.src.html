Components

A reference document, not part of either app: every component of the Executive Frame Learning Hub and Client Hub, drawn by each app's own CSS and markup, in every state it has. It shows both apps as they are after brand pack 1.1 went live on 12 November 2026, side by side; what still differs, and what is open with the brand designer, is under Differences to settle. The guide as it stood before the Learning Hub was aligned on 10 November is components-before-alignment-2026-11-10.html beside this file. All names and content are sample data.

Colour and type

Both apps take their palette, themes, application colours, spacing, radius and motion from the brand pack's brand/ef-tokens.json (version 1.1, 12 November 2026), written into each app's tokens.css by a script. 4 palette colours, Figtree at 300, 400 and 500. Type sizes and control sizes are each app's own and match. 1 value does not follow the pack on purpose: the primary button is navy, not slate. Values below are read live from each app's CSS.

Status, tags and notices

A tag or status is a label, never a button. Since brand pack 1.1 both apps draw them the same way: .status is a 16 px stroked icon and a word on the tint of its kind (done is the pack's success colour, in progress its info colour, attention its warning colour), and .tag is a plain warm white pill with a hairline for anything neutral, such as Not started. A notice is 1 component with an icon and a word. Nothing is shown by colour alone.

Recognise

Before you start

The Reset is self directed education. It teaches a method for thinking under load. It is not treatment, and it does not replace support from a health professional.

It may not be the right time if:

  • You are in the middle of a crisis, or your safety is at risk.
  • You are finding it hard to get through the day at the moment.

Is now a good time to work through this on your own?

That is a sound call. This will be here when you want it.

The Learning Hub is education, not therapy. If things are hard right now, call or text 1737 at any time, free, to talk with a trained counsellor. In an emergency, call 111. You can also contact Executive Frame at consultant@sarahanticich.co.nz.

Open with the brand designer

From the 2 brand pack records: places where an app does not follow the guidelines and nothing was changed, because the decision is not Charlotte's alone. The decisions that stand are not in this list: the primary button is navy, body text is 400, there is no dark mode in this release, and the installed apps are named Learning Hub and Client Hub.

  • Quiet ink colour. The Learning Hub's --ink-quiet #445C86 is a 5th colour for secondary text; the guidelines say there is no muted text colour. The Client Hub's softened slate for placeholder text is the same kind of thing.
  • 6 px progress bar. Both apps. The guidelines ask for a thin hairline bar.
  • Captions at 400. Both apps. The guidelines set captions and body at Light; body stays 400 by decision and captions follow it.
  • Centred loading screens. Both apps centre the mark and its line (and the Learning Hub its cover and step count). The guidelines say left align everything.
  • Navy theme colour. The pack's head snippet sets theme-color to navy, so a phone browser's top bar is navy above a sky page. Followed as supplied in both.
  • The lesson's loop diagram. Learning Hub. The frame step draws its own ring, triangle and labelled points; the guidelines call the mark the only drawn element. With Sarah as well.
  • The client type accents. Client Hub. 2 colours outside the palette, kept as a deliberate exception (Charlotte, 12 November 2026); the guidelines say no colour per pathway.
  • Also in the records. White as a hover fill; status colours on lesson cards and on the tracker (the guidelines keep application colours to forms and messages); no logo in the Learning Hub's phone header; Return shown as a 4th stage of the loop (Sarah); sky is provisional in the pack; the Client Hub's line icons, the exclamation mark drawn in 2 icons, and capitals in the activation code box.
