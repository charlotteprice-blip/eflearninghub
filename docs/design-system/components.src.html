Components

A reference document, not part of either app: every component of the Executive Frame Learning Hub and Client Hub, drawn by each app's own CSS and markup, in every state it has. It shows the 2 apps as they are today, side by side, so the differences can be seen and 1 version chosen; all names and content are sample data.

Recognise

Before you start

The Reset is self directed education. It teaches a method for thinking under load. It is not treatment, and it does not replace support from a health professional.

It may not be the right time if:

  • You are in the middle of a crisis, or your safety is at risk.
  • You are finding it hard to get through the day at the moment.

Is now a good time to work through this on your own?

That is a sound call. This will be here when you want it.

The Learning Hub is education, not therapy. If things are hard right now, call or text 1737 at any time, free, to talk with a trained counsellor. In an emergency, call 111. You can also contact Executive Frame at contact email to be confirmed.
