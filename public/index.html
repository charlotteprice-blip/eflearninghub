      Help and support

      • Choose a topic in the library on the left. It opens one step at a time.
      • Move on with Next and Back, or the left and right arrow keys.
      • In the C³ Loop, drag the triangle to turn it, or select a point.
      • In sorting exercises, drag a moment into a group, or select it and then select the group.
      • Your place, notes and reflections are kept in this browser only, so they stay on this device.
      • Downloads are in each topic and in Notes and files. During a free trial the number of downloads is limited.
      • Billing, your plan and your login are on My account.

      For anything else, contact Executive Frame at contact email to be confirmed.
