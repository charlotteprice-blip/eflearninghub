Privacy statement

Draft for approval, version 2026-10-18

Who we are

The Executive Frame Learning Hub is run by Executive Frame, the practice of Sarah Anticich, a registered psychologist in Christchurch. This statement covers the Learning Hub at app.executiveframe.co.nz. It explains what we keep, where it is kept and why, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

What we keep

Your name and email address, how you sign in (Google, Microsoft, or email and password), your membership and its dates, the arm you joined through, the organisation that provides your place if you have one, how many files you have downloaded during a free trial, and when you accepted this statement.

Your card details go directly to Stripe. They never reach the Learning Hub, and we cannot see your full card number.

What stays on your device

Your place in each topic, your answers to exercises and anything you write in reflection boxes are kept in your browser only. They are not sent to Executive Frame, and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser's data removes them.

Where it is stored

Google (Sydney). Your login and membership records are stored by Google through Firebase, in Google Cloud's Sydney region in Australia.

Stripe. Payments, subscriptions, receipts and your billing details are handled and stored by Stripe, which may store them outside New Zealand.

Workshop videos are hosted by a video provider, such as YouTube or Vimeo. The video only loads, and the provider only receives information from your browser, when you press play.

Why we keep it

To give you access to the Learning Hub, to manage your membership and payments, and to give organisations the number of places they have arranged. If your organisation provides your place, it learns that a place has been used. It does not see which topics you open or anything you write.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

While your account is open, and for as long afterwards as tax and accounting law requires for payment records. You can ask us to close your account and delete your login at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Contact us at contact email to be confirmed. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
